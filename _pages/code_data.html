Software I develop or contribute to. Most of it targets integral-field spectroscopy with Keck/KCWI and multi-band modeling of AGN host galaxies.

KCWI · post-processing
KcwiKit

Post-processing tools for the Keck Cosmic Web Imager (ASCL 2404.003). I wrote kcwi_xalign (frame alignment and blue/red channel cross-alignment) and kcwi_skylevel (matching sky levels between the blue and red channels).

KCWI · sky subtraction
KSkyWizard

Improves the reduction of KCWI red-channel data: sky subtraction, flux calibration and telluric correction. I contributed bug fixes and used it on the KCWI data for UGC 2369S.

Imaging + SED fitting
GalfitS

Python package that fits multi-band images and SEDs together to separate AGN and host-galaxy light. It uses JAX just-in-time compilation and MCMC for uncertainties. I help develop it.

X-ray reflection
XILLVER / XSTAR

Reflection and photoionization models for accreting black holes. I implemented the high-density plasma corrections to the atomic rates described in Ding et al. 2024.
