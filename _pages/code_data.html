Much of my work depends on community software for integral-field spectroscopy and X-ray spectral modeling. These are the packages I have contributed to. My contributions to each are partial: the main credit goes to their developers, who are linked below.

KCWI data reduction
KcwiKit

Post-processing tools for the Keck Cosmic Web Imager, developed by N. Prusinski, Y. Chen and collaborators (ASCL 2404.003). I have contributed small additions for aligning exposures and matching the blue and red channels.

KCWI data reduction
KSkyWizard

Sky subtraction and calibration for KCWI red-channel data, developed by Z. Zhuang. I have made minor contributions, mostly bug fixes, from using it on my own data.

X-ray reflection
XILLVER / XSTAR

Reflection and photoionization models for accreting black holes, developed by J. García, T. Kallman and collaborators. I contributed updates to the atomic rates for high-density plasma effects (Ding et al. 2024).
