Deep integral-field data are only as good as their reduction. Much of my thesis work rests on Keck/KCWI data, and I have put substantial effort into improving the community tools used to reduce it. The original developers built the foundation of each package below. My contributions extend that foundation, aiming to make deep KCWI stacks as clean as possible, especially for faint, extended Lyα emission.

KCWI data reduction

Reduction pipeline
KCWI DRP

The official Python data reduction pipeline for KCWI (docs). My improvements to the reduction:

  • Better cosmic-ray rejection
  • More accurate wavelength correction
  • Automated generation of sky and source masks
Post-processing
KcwiKit

Post-processing and stacking for KCWI, originally developed by Y. Chen, N. Prusinski and collaborators (ASCL 2404.003). My additions:

  • Much more accurate frame-to-frame and blue/red cross-channel alignment
  • Post-DRP cosmic-ray rejection that removes nearly all CR residuals
  • Post-DRP sky matching that removes sky-subtraction differences between channels
Red-channel sky subtraction
KSkyWizard

Sky subtraction and calibration for the KCWI red channel, originally developed by Z. Zhuang. My additions:

  • Automated processing
  • Bug fixes

X-ray reflection models

Spectral models
XILLVER / XSTAR

Reflection and photoionization models for accreting black holes, developed by J. García, T. Kallman and collaborators. I implemented the high-density plasma corrections to the atomic rates (Ding et al. 2024).
