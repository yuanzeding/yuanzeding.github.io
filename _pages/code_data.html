Deep integral-field data are only as good as their reduction. My thesis rests on Keck/KCWI data of faint, extended Lyα emission and of nearby AGN hosts, so I have put substantial work into the tools that turn raw KCWI exposures into science-ready cubes and maps. The original developers of each package built its foundation. My contributions extend it, aiming to make deep blue+red KCWI stacks as clean and as well registered as possible.

Per-frame reduction
KCWI DRP

Built on the official Keck KCWI pipeline. My work on the per-frame reduction:

  • Cosmic-ray rejection that no longer flags bright continuum sources (QSOs, galaxy nuclei) as cosmic rays
  • Appropriate handling of the wavelength corrections
  • Automated sky and source masks from source contours, for every exposure, replacing hand-drawn regions
Alignment & stacking
KcwiKit

Built on KcwiKit by Y. Chen, N. Prusinski and collaborators (ASCL 2404.003). My additions:

  • Joint frame-to-frame and blue/red cross-channel alignment by cross-correlation. It needs no point source and works across position angles, registering both channels to ~0.01–0.02″.
  • Post-DRP cosmic-ray rejection that compares each frame with the rest and removes nearly all CR residuals
  • Post-DRP sky-level matching that removes sky-subtraction differences between the blue and red channels
Red-channel sky
KSkyWizard

Telluric correction and PCA sky subtraction for the KCWI red channel, developed by Z. Zhuang. My additions:

  • Automated processing (the auto branch)
  • Bug fixes
IFU spectral fitting
GIST for KCWI blue+red

Extends the GIST pipeline (Bittner et al. 2019) for pPXF fits of combined blue+red KCWI cubes, in a development version that is not yet public:

  • A corrected blue+red read-in: proper wavelength grid across the dichroic gap, plus mask and variance propagation
  • Hierarchical spatial binning that follows surface brightness (Pipe3D/CAPIVARA-style), as an alternative to Voronoi
  • Mask-aware S/N, a covariance check for drizzled cubes, and a number of robustness fixes

X-ray reflection models

Spectral models
XILLVER / XSTAR

Reflection and photoionization models for accreting black holes, developed by J. García, T. Kallman and collaborators. I implemented the high-density plasma corrections to the atomic rates (Ding et al. 2024).
